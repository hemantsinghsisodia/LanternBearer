## Readability
Rule 1 check (edges and creatures visible at default brightness) on the after-set cliff, shoreline, water and moth images of Island1-4, plus the Shade shot on Island4, Low and Ultra (17 shots x 2 presets), viewed in side-by-side contact sheets.

**Failures: none.** Cliffs, shorelines, the moth and the Shade are distinguishable from their surroundings on both presets. Observations, none a failure:
- Moth: dark silhouette against the lantern glow and halo on Ultra, dark blocky wings on Low. Visible on all islands (`island1/ultra/moth.jpg`, `island1/low/moth.jpg`).
- Shade (`island4/low/shade.jpg`, `island4/ultra/shade.jpg`): readable against the sky and water rim, eyes visible on Ultra. The lower body merges with the dark ground on Ultra, so it relies on the rim and eyes.
- Water on Low is a near uniform dark blue (`island2/low/water.jpg`, `island3/low/water.jpg`, `island4/low/water.jpg`). Shoreline and horizon edges still read, but the surface has little detail. Ultra water shows fine speckle and mist patches.
- Ultra mist on the water close-ups is bright and soft (`island2/ultra/water.jpg`, `island4/ultra/water.jpg`). It does not hide edges.
- Island4 cliff walls are flat grey against the purple sky on both presets, readable via the top edge (`island4/low/cliff.jpg`).
