### What the profile found
- The fix made (cliff skin stops casting shadows; Island 5 only, installed by the new `Island5PerfInstaller` as the third `BuildIsland5` post step) removed 0.8-1.2M triangles, 33-50 shadow casters and about 35 draw calls in the editor and the player, with no visible change in before/after shots (beach, mid-climb, far sea). It did not lower the frame time.
- Editor GPU timing (Game view 1920x1080, render scale 1.5, Profiler GPU area on) shows geometry is not the bottleneck: removing the cladding, the skin, the props, the grass, the beacons, the sky or the shadow casting of any group changes the GPU frame by 0.5 ms or less. Only the terrain matters (about 6 ms of 14 ms), and the terrain is not unusual in kind (all islands use the same 5-layer shader).
- The editor shows Island 4 heavier than Island 5 at the same settings and pose (Island 4 about 19.8 ms, Island 5 about 13.8 ms GPU, before the fix). So Island 5 is not intrinsically heavier than Island 4.
- The comparison itself is not like for like. The Phase G baseline (Islands 1-4) was measured before the render scale setting existed (commit 6f54b7c, after the Phase G re-measure), so it ran at render scale 1.0. The player's saved settings now hold `LanternKeeperRenderScale = 1.5`, and both the H1 probe and this re-measure applied 1.50 on Ultra and 0.75 on Low (see `applied=renderScale` in the raw files). That is 2.25x the pixels of the baseline, and the Ultra delta (10.95 / 5.11 = 2.14x) and Low delta (1.5x) match it. A fair gate needs the Islands 1-4 baseline re-measured at 1.5, or Island 5 measured at render scale 1.0. That was not done here (single-pass rule; the player's settings were left as the user has them).
- Run-to-run noise is large on this machine (this re-measure's Low CPU frame rose 51% although it draws 45% fewer triangles), so the after numbers cannot be read as a regression caused by the change; the power plan and thermal state were not controlled.
