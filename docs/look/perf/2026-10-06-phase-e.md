## Gate (average frame time; limit: no island or preset more than 0.3 ms slower than D2)
- Ultra budget (avg <= 12.5 ms): worst is 5.43 ms (Island1). PASS.
- Regression limit +0.3 ms: **FAIL on Island4 Low (+0.55, re-runs +0.66 and +0.70) and Island4 Ultra (+0.39; re-runs +0.47 and +0.21).** All other islands and presets are within the limit (worst +0.27, Island3 Ultra). No tuning was done; reported for a decision. Probe noise is about 0.5 ms, but the Island4 Low shift is repeatable. The Phase E changes are menu and UI only, so a world-cost cause is not expected; the main-menu scene or a shared setting may be involved.
