## Gate
- Ultra budget (avg <= 12.5 ms): worst is 5.58 ms (Island3). PASS.
- Regression limit +0.3 ms versus F3: **FAIL on 4 of 8 runs** (Island2 Ultra +0.77, Island3 Low +0.55, Island3 Ultra +0.51, Island4 Ultra +0.56). All eight runs are slower than F3 (+0.17 to +0.77 ms), so this is a consistent shift, not one outlier. Probe noise was about 0.5 ms in earlier phases. Per the single-pass rule nothing was re-run or investigated; the cause (audio main-thread cost such as source updates and scheduler code, versus noise or machine state) is unknown.
