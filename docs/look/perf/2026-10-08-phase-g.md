## Gate (first pass)
- Ultra budget (avg <= 12.5 ms): worst is 5.58 ms (Island3). PASS.
- Regression limit +0.3 ms versus F3: **FAIL on 4 of 8 runs** (Island2 Ultra +0.77, Island3 Low +0.55, Island3 Ultra +0.51, Island4 Ultra +0.56). All eight runs are slower than F3 (+0.17 to +0.77 ms), so this is a consistent shift, not one outlier. Probe noise was about 0.5 ms in earlier phases. ### Audio profile (editor, Island3, Ultra, 300 frames, standing at spawn, intros marked seen)
- Main-thread audio work is not measurable: `AudioManager.Update`, `Audio.FMODSystemUpdate`, `PostLateUpdate.UpdateAudio`, `AudioSource.Update` and the streaming-decoder markers all read 0 ms per frame. Reflection micro-benchmarks of the Update methods: AudioManager 0.2 us, MusicDirector 0.3 us, MusicPlayer 0.05 us, Tide 0.16 us, MothSpawner 0.4 us, Moth 6.5 us per call; `MusicPlayer.IsMuted` 0.02 us. The sum is far below 0.01 ms per frame, so the scripts are not the regression.
- Sources at spawn, all beacons unlit: Island3 has 26 AudioSources, 7 to 8 playing (music, tension at volume 0, island bed, tide, Lantern crackle, 2 moth flutter loops, moth whisper when a moth is near). Island2 has 25, 6 playing (music, tension at volume 0, island bed, crackle, 2 moth flutter loops). The pool, UI, heartbeat and idle sources are stopped.
- Audio threads (off the main thread), per audio-thread call: stream thread 0.142 ms before, 0.042 ms after the in-memory change (about 3x lower, 10 down to 3 calls per profiler frame); mixer thread 0.28 ms before, 0.23 ms after. These were taken in two editor sessions with different focus state, so treat them as indicative only.
- Voice settings: real 32, virtual 512 (Unity defaults), 7 to 8 playing, so no change.
- Hypotheses not supported: per-frame mixer `SetFloat` (already change-gated, now also 0.05 dB gated), voice pool (12 idle sources), `GetOutputData` (never called).
