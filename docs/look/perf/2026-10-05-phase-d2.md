Build: Windows 64-bit, non-development, Burst AOT off, built through the Unity MCP build action from branch `phase-d2-props` (HEAD fc3d091). Output `Builds/PerfD2/LanternKeeper.exe` (ignored, `Builds/` is git-ignored). Build time 18 s, 249 MB (D1: 204 s, 654 MB; the Poly Haven textures are gone and shader variants were cached).

Same method as `2026-10-03-phase-b.md`: vsync off, 3 s warm-up, 20 s walk-forward sample. Machine: RTX 4050 Laptop GPU, Direct3D 11, 1920x1080 fullscreen. Power mode and plugged-in state were not verified, as before. The Unity editor was open and idle during the runs.

## Gate (average frame time is the gated metric; p95 is recorded only)
- Ultra budget: average <= 12.5 ms. Worst Ultra average is 5.60 ms (Island1). **PASS** on all four islands (headroom 6.9 ms).
- Low versus D1 (limit +1 ms): worst is Island1 at +0.61 ms. **PASS** on all four islands. The probe noise is about 0.5 ms, so +0.61 is only slightly above noise; the other three Low islands moved by -0.30 to +0.02 ms.
- Ultra p95 improved or held on every island (7.24 to 9.45 ms).
- Island1's median triangle count fell from 2.85M to 0.74M (the photo-scanned trees were heavier than the MegaKit trees). Low runs at renderScale 0.5, 387 to 507 fps on the RTX 4050.
