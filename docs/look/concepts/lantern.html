Which lantern does the keeper carry?

All three hang from a bail (handle) held in the keeper's fist, swing like a pendulum, and have a real flame whose size follows fuel. They're shown at full and low fuel. The shape needs to read as a silhouette in the dark and look good up close on the HUD icon.

A · Hurricane lantern

A rounded glass globe in a wire guard, with a fuel tank base and a wire bail. It's the classic, instantly recognisable, soft-glowing lantern. Its round silhouette is friendly but common.

Chosen

B · Iron storm lantern (recommended)

A square forged-iron frame with four glass panes, a peaked roof cap and a ring handle. The tall, angular shape reads crisply at a distance and as a HUD icon. The panes split the light into four soft beams, which suits the "keeper" story and echoes the Phase 3 lighthouse.

C · Brass ship's lantern

A cylindrical brass body with ribbed glass and a ring top. Warm and nautical, with brass that catches the light nicely. Rounder and busier in silhouette, and it reads more "ship" than "wanderer".

Shared behaviour (any choice)

The flame shrinks and reddens as fuel drops. The glass glow pulses with the flicker, and the light sits at the flame. The lantern swings with walking and wind and dims when moths are near. It also becomes the HUD fuel icon in Phase F.
