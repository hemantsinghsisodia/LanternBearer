How should the Shades be rendered?

The hollow-wraith shape stays. This is about treatment: how it reads in darkness, in your lantern's light (frozen), and in a lightning flash (stunned). Today it's a flat, dark, semi-transparent shape.

Chosen

A · Smoke wraith (recommended)

A near-opaque, ink-dark body. The edges are noisy and ragged, and break into smoke at the hem, which trails behind it. The cloak tatters sway through a shader. Pinprick eyes glow softly. A lightning flash gives a crisp pale-blue outline. In your lantern's light it freezes and its edges flicker, as if it's burning away.

B · Void cutout

Pure black that absorbs light, like a hole cut in the world, with a faint warping of the background at its edges. It's striking and graphic, but in balanced darkness against a dark sky it can vanish entirely, and it relies on the warping effect, which is costlier.

C · Spectral ghost

Pale, translucent and softly glowing, fading out toward the hem. Always visible, but it reads as a classic ghost, which is less scary, and it clashes with "they live in the dark and fear your light".
