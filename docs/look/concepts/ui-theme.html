A · Forged iron and amber (recommended)

Deep ink panels framed in thin dark-brass lines. Lantern-amber marks the primary action and the glow. A Cormorant Garamond serif for titles, an italic Spectral for flavour text, and Inter for numbers and buttons. HUD icons share the storm-lantern shape language. Atmospheric but very readable, and it works for menus, HUD and journal alike.

B · Weathered journal

Everything is ink on parchment, with IM Fell English throughout and HUD notes written like journal entries. Very characterful and great for the Keeper's Log. But bright paper panels at night fight the darkness and the lantern glow, and the HUD gets hard to read at a glance in the middle of gameplay.

C · Minimal glass

Translucent frosted panels, an all-caps Inter sans, and thin ring gauges. Modern, clean and very readable, but it feels like a generic app UI and has none of the "keeper and lantern" personality the painterly world now has.

Whichever you pick

The Keeper's Log can still use a parchment journal spread as a special screen, even under A or C.
