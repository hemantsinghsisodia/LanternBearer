Who is the keeper? Silhouette concepts

In balanced darkness the keeper mostly reads as a silhouette against the lantern glow, so the shape matters more than detail. Each figure holds a real lantern by its handle (bail), not glued to the wrist. The current model is shown for reference.

Today (reference)

Generic low-poly figure in flat orange. Nothing says "keeper".

Chosen

A · Hooded wanderer (recommended)

Deep hood with a face lost in shadow, a long cloak with a torn hem, and a satchel. The lantern is held out at arm's length by its handle. Mysterious and simple: a lone figure carrying light. It rhymes with the Shades without copying them, and the warm cloak rim catches the lantern.

B · Lighthouse keeper

Oilskin coat, wide-brim rain hat, a red scarf, heavy boots. A grounded, human, nautical figure that ties into the Phase 3 lighthouse finale. Warmer and more relatable, less mysterious.

C · Young apprentice

A small, child-like keeper with a trailing scarf, a short cape and an oversized lantern. Vulnerable and charming, so the darkness feels bigger by contrast (think Little Nightmares or Alba). The scarf animates nicely in wind gusts.
