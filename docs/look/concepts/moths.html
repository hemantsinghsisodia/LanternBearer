What should the moths look like?

Each panel shows a moth far from the lantern (left) and one draining you (right, within 1.5 m). Violet stays the "draining" colour, matching the HUD. Wings flap through a shader, so no rig is needed. Today they're black discs with a halo.

Chosen

A · Dusty moth (recommended)

Pale, dusty wings with soft markings and a dark fuzzy body. Far away it's a dim, grey-blue flutter. Up close its wings catch your lantern and glow warm, so it's clearly a moth and clearly attracted to your light. A faint violet aura and falling wing dust show it's draining.

B · Spirit moth

Translucent, faintly glowing wings, always visible in the dark. Easy to track, but it competes with the fireflies (also glowing points of light) and makes moths feel magical rather than threatening.

C · Ash moth

Soot-black wings with violet ember eyes; only the lantern-lit wing edges glow. It's menacing and matches the Shades, but it's nearly invisible in balanced darkness until it's already on you. That's harder to read.

Shared (any choice)

Shader wing flap, jittery fluttering flight, a violet draining aura and falling wing dust, and they keep the same gameplay (range, drain, avoidance).
