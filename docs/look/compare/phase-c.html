Phase C: keeper and lantern, before and after

Left is the keeper before Phase C (keeper/2026-10-03-before), right is the Phase C result (keeper/2026-10-03-after). Same studio scene and shot list, 1920x1080, JPEG q92, on Low and Ultra. Three shots (front, threequarter, back) in three states (idle, gust, lowfuel).

What changed in Phase C:

  • True-scale keeper (1.86 m) with a hooded cloak, belt, satchel and boots, approved from the Blender renders below.
  • Authored KeeperLit materials with a slate-blue cloak, a dark face inside the hood and cloak sway.
  • Storm lantern on the hand socket, hanging straight down, with a fuel-driven flame and a glass glow.
  • Animator layers (arm, lean, actions, upper-body actions) driven by game events, and footstep dust at the feet (not visible in these static shots).
