Phase F1: before and after

Left is the Phase F1 starting point (baseline/2026-10-07-f1-before), right is the result (baseline/2026-10-07-f1-after). Same shot lists, 1920x1080, JPEG, Low and Ultra, captured in the editor Game view. Moth, beacon and Shade shots change; spawn, cliff, shoreline, water and grass are expected to be unchanged.

What changed in Phase F1:

  • Modelled moth (0.4 m wingspan) with shader-flapped wings, a violet drain aura and wing dust.
  • Storm-lantern beacon on a dry-stone cairn: dark unlit glass, amber lit panes, beams and a ground-hugging 8 m ring.
  • Lighting moment: flare, 1 s ramp, embers, a soft "whoomp" and a ring bloom.
  • Smoke-wraith Shade with a lightning outline and an ash-ember freeze burn.
  • Gameplay is unchanged; every effect has a Low variant.
