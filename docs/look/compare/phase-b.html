Phase B: before and after

Left is the Phase A baseline (baseline/2026-10-03), right is the Phase B result (baseline/2026-10-03-b). Same shot lists, 1920x1080, JPEG q92, on Low and Ultra.

What changed in Phase B:

  • Cold moonlight with a painterly moon rim that keeps edges readable at night (always on, simpler on Low).
  • Per-island look volumes: grading, bloom, vignette, SSAO and light film grain by quality preset.
  • Painted night sky with a moon disc lined up with the moonlight, plus a dawn grade.
  • Ground mist on the higher presets.
  • Amber lantern glow with a halo. The reveal radius is unchanged.
