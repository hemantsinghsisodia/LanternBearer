Phase D1: before and after

Left is the D1 starting point (baseline/2026-10-05-d1-before), right is the Phase D1 result (baseline/2026-10-05-d1-after). Same shot lists, 1920x1080, JPEG, on Low and Ultra.

What changed in Phase D1:

  • Painterly water: flat tinted surface, foam line and water-line rim that follow the tide, always on in Low.
  • Painted terrain layers generated from island palettes (no photographic textures).
  • Cool, sparse grass with a root-to-tip palette.
  • Flat silhouette ridges with a palette haze, blending toward dawn.
