Phase D2: before and after

Left is the D2 starting point (baseline/2026-10-05-d2-before), right is the Phase D2 result (baseline/2026-10-05-d2-after). Same shot lists, 1920x1080, JPEG, on Low and Ultra. The after shots were captured in the editor Game view.

What changed in Phase D2:

  • Stylized Nature MegaKit props (Quaternius, CC0) replace the photo-scanned Poly Haven trees, bushes, flowers and rocks. Biome counts and categories are kept.
  • Generated deadwood and reed tufts.
  • Island 4 tree tint driven by the island profile, so the leaves are no longer a saturated green.
  • Cliff cladding rocks (2 to 6 m) placed against steep faces, with no colliders.
  • Triplanar rock-wall shading on the terrain (LanternKeeper/TerrainLit): steep faces use the island rock layer projected from the side, so there is no vertical stretching.
  • Cliff skin mesh traced along the steep steps, smoothed in plan, which hides the jagged top edge of the heightmap walls. Terrain heights stay byte-identical.

Known residuals: small 0.1 to 0.3 m sawtooth teeth remain on some path banks (visible at Island 1 spawn); the Island 4 tree hue is close to Island 1's but much darker; trails that hug a wall clip the skin by about 0.25 m.

Review of the after shots (spawn, cliff, beacon, all islands, Low and Ultra):

  • Island 1: dark green conifers and bushes read clearly; cliff faces are grey rock cladding with a smooth skin behind, top edge tidy. At spawn the path bank still shows a sawtooth strip.
  • Island 2: warm ochre, golden foliage and bare dead trees; the cliff is a dense stack of grey rocks. Reads well, a little busy.
  • Island 3: olive and green broadleaf trees; the cliff is a pale blue-grey wall with cladding rocks and a visible waterline. Hue is distinct from Island 2.
  • Island 4: dark blue-grey rocks under a purple sky, purple grass, near-black trees. Darker overall than the other islands; the cliff reads as flat grey slabs.
  • Low shows the same props as Ultra with fewer grass blades and less shadow; no readability failures seen.
