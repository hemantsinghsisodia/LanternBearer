Phase F3: before and after

Left is the Phase F3 starting point (baseline/2026-10-08-f2-after, there is no separate f3-before set), right is the result (baseline/2026-10-08-f3-after). Same shot lists, 1920x1080, JPEG, Low and Ultra, captured in the editor Game view. What changed in Phase F3:

  • The placeholder firefly is replaced by a blinking swarm with mote cores and a green halo.
  • Collected swarms stream into the lantern, linger, and kick the lantern light.
  • Gameplay rules are unchanged.
