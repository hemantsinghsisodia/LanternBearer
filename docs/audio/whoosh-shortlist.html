Lantern Keeper beacon fire whoosh shortlist

How to pick: reply with a number, for example Whoosh 1. Click a title to open its page and press play there. Recommendation: Whoosh 1, Gasoline fire #1.

Replaces the current Beacon_Ignite_* (from 80 CC0 RPG SFX by rubberduck, so that pack is not re-suggested). Wanted: a warm ignition whoosh and flare of a big storm lantern catching, about 0.6-2.0 s, with a soft crackling tail; plays dozens of times per island, so nothing harsh, piercing or comedic. All candidates were checked against the licence shown on their own page; each lists CC0. Nothing was downloaded, so trim points are estimates to confirm by ear. Note: no CC0 clip on these sites is a ready-made 1 s lantern whoosh, so most picks need a trim and a short fade.

Beacon ignite

Warm "whump" of fuel catching, short flare, quiet crackle on the decay. Apply a gentle low-pass if the source is bright.
