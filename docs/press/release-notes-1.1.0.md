# Lantern Keeper 1.1.0: The Great Light

The keeper's journey now has an ending. Island 5 is a storm-wrapped mountain with a dark lighthouse at its summit. Relight it, and the game closes on its first sunrise.

## New island: The Great Light
- **A spiral climb.** A path winds about 1¾ times round the mountain, through three height bands, from the beach to a lighthouse 40 m up. There are 8 beacons to light on the way.
- **Light bridges.** Four gaps are too wide to jump. Light the beacon at the edge and a bridge of light forms to carry you across. The last bridge opens only when all eight beacons burn.
- **The storm only grows.** The higher you climb, the harder the island fights back, and turning back won't calm it:
  - the low band brings moths;
  - the middle band brings wind, Shades and lightning;
  - the top brings thin air, which shrinks the lantern's light and drains it faster.
- **A rising storm.** A dark surge climbs the mountain behind you. Inside it, your lantern drains three times faster and beacon light can't shelter you. Every beacon you light pushes it back down.
- **Lightning that hunts you.** A glowing ring marks where the next strike will land. Keep moving, or shelter on a bridge or in a lit beacon's light.
- **Scarce fuel.** The island has fewer fireflies, beacons give only a little fuel back, and the lantern burns faster. Plan your route.
- **The Keeper's Log** gains three new pages that tell the lighthouse's story.
- **Island 5 unlocks** once Island 4 is won.

## The finale
- **The great lamp ignites,** with flame, embers and a sweeping beam.
- **A 66-second flight.** The storm breaks open, and the camera sweeps past Islands 1–4 as each one's beacons flare in turn. Then it turns to the first sunrise over a blue-gold sea, with light breaking through the clouds.
- **An original finale score:** "Fantasy Orchestral Theme" by Joth (CC0).
- **A credits roll** over the dawn, with every CC0 source credited. Any key skips it.

## Changes across the game
- **A dark lighthouse on the horizon.** Islands 1–4 now show the Great Light's tower, dark, because the storm put it out. In the main menu it lights up once you've won Island 5.
- **A growing pre-dawn.** Winning Islands 1–4 brings a pre-dawn that grows a little brighter on each island. The full sunrise is saved for the finale.
- **A new beacon sound.** Lighting a beacon plays a new recorded fire ignition.
- **Each island in the menu shows the beacons you've lit,** and a won island shows them all.

## Fixes
- You can no longer get stuck in the rocks near the Island 5 summit, and a safety net frees the keeper if he's ever wedged.
- Pressing R during the credits no longer restarts the island.
- The finale's beacon light columns stay whole over the sea.
- The HUD hides during the finale.
- The low-fuel warning effects stop once you win.
- Synthesised sounds no longer drop out after changing scenes.
